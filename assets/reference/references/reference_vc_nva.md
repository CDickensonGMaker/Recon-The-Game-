# VC / NVA Fighter Visual Reference (1960–1972)
### For 4–6 low-poly PS1-style character variants — Hell of Duty

Sources: militaria dealers (Enemy Militaria, IMA-USA, Vintage Productions, Hessen Antique),
reenactor guides (3rd NVA Division living history, Heritage Airsoft), vietnamgear.com,
militarysunhelmets.com, HistoryNet/GlobalSecurity sapper articles, KommandoStore/Kruschiki
Chicom rig listings, Wikipedia (khăn rằn, áo bà bà).

---

## 0. THE BIG PICTURE — silhouettes first

At PS1 resolution the six variants must read by **silhouette + 2 colors**. The instant-read keys:

| Variant | Silhouette key | Color key |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Local VC farmer | Cone hat, baggy loose clothes, no gear bulk | All near-black |
| 2. Main-force VC | Boonie/soft hat + chest rig bulge | Mixed khaki top / black bottom |
| 3. Sapper | Near-naked, shorts only, satchel in hand | Bare skin dominates |
| 4. Female VC | Cone hat OR long hair/braid + checkered scarf | Black + white-check scarf |
| 5. NVA regular | Pith helmet dome + rucksack hump | Uniform single-color green/khaki |
| 6. Heavy weapons | RPG tube / RPD + rocket backpack spikes | Same as 5 but lumpy silhouette |

All are **short and lean** compared to US GIs — this is a real gameplay-visible difference (see §9).

## 1. LOCAL-FORCE VC FARMER-GUERRILLA ("black pajamas")

The part-time village fighter. Indistinguishable from a farmer until armed.

**Clothing — áo bà ba ("black pajamas")**
- Two-piece: collarless pullover or button-front shirt (long sleeve, side slits at hip)
  + drawstring loose trousers. Very baggy, thin cotton — flaps in wind, no taper.
- "Black" is rarely true black. The cloth was home-dyed and sun-faded fast:
  - Fresh dye: very dark blue-black `#232830` or brown-black `#2b2724`
  - Typical faded: charcoal with blue cast `#33363c`
  - Heavily worn: washed-out gray-brown `#4a4844`
  - Militaria examples also come in dark indigo `#2a3240` and muted brown `#463a2e`
  - PS1 tip: use `#2e3138` base with `#44484f` highlight so it doesn't crush to silhouette-black.
- Trousers often rolled to mid-calf (paddy work habit).

**Headgear**
- Nón lá conical straw hat (see §8.4) — the classic read. Alternate: bare head or a
  dark scarf tied as headband.

**Footwear**
- Ho Chi Minh tire sandals (§8.5) or barefoot. Dark feet.

**Gear** — minimal:
- One small cloth shoulder bag (rice sock or musette) slung diagonally, tan/olive.
- Maybe a single stick grenade in waistband, water gourd or French-era canteen on rope.
- NO chest rig — that's the main-force marker.

**Weapon**: bolt-action (Mosin M44, French MAS-36, or captured M1 carbine), or old SKS.
Weapon held low, non-military posture works for this unit.

**Build**: wiry, slightly stooped, sun-dark. 1.55–1.62 m.

**3–5 m read**: cone hat + all-dark baggy clothes + long old rifle + no webbing.

## 2. MAIN-FORCE VC REGULAR

Full-time fighter in battalion-strength units. Semi-uniformed — the "mix and match" look.

**Clothing**
- Deliberately mixed: khaki or gray-green shirt (often NVA-supplied or captured) over
  black pajama trousers, or all-black with military web gear. Cut is still loose but a
  proper button shirt with two chest pockets.
- VC khaki: dusty yellow-tan `#9a8a64`, faded `#a89a76`
- Gray-green alternative shirt: `#6f7462`
- Black trousers as §1: `#2e3138`

**Headgear**
- Most iconic: **soft boonie/jungle hat** ("VC bush hat") in faded olive `#6b6b52`, floppy
  full brim, shallow round crown. Also seen: fiber/pith helmet (NVA-supplied, §8.3),
  or bare head with khăn rằn scarf. For variant clarity give this one the **boonie**,
  save the pith helmet for NVA.

**Footwear**: Ho Chi Minh sandals; some had captured US "Bata boots" or sneakers. Sandals fine.

**Gear** — this is the equipped one:
- **Chicom Type 56 3-pocket chest rig** (§8.1) — THE identifying item. Worn high on the chest.
- Canvas water canteen on waist belt or rope sling (dull aluminum w/ olive cover `#5c5f4a`).
- 1–2 stick grenades tucked in rig side pockets, wooden handles showing.
- Rolled rice tube (bag of rice sewn into a sausage) worn diagonally across torso —
  great cheap silhouette element: a fabric roll from left shoulder to right hip, tan `#8c7f5c`.

**Weapon**: AK-47 / Type 56 (folding spike bayonet), or SKS.

**Build**: fit adult male, 1.60–1.65 m, lean.

**3–5 m read**: boonie hat + chest rig bulge + rice roll across chest + AK. Khaki-over-black
color split vs the farmer's all-dark.

## 3. VC SAPPER / ASSAULT (đặc công)

Elite night-raid demolition troops. Attacked wire perimeters nearly naked — bare skin let
them feel trip wires and mines, and greased skin slipped through wire.

**Clothing**
- **Shorts only** — black or dark olive cotton shorts (militaria: "VC sapper shorts"),
  sometimes just a loincloth. `#26282a`.
- Bare torso, often smeared with charcoal/grease — darken skin tone with gray-black
  streaks `#3a332c` on shoulders/face.
- No shirt, no hat. Maybe a dark cloth headband `#1f1f1f`.

**Footwear**: barefoot (historically) or tire sandals. Barefoot sells the variant.

**Gear** — stripped to explosives:
- **Satchel charges**: rectangular canvas bags (~30×20×8 cm) with a shoulder strap —
  one in hand, 1–2 slung across the back. Canvas tan/olive `#7a7157` with dark strap.
- Wire cutters on a cord, a knife at the waist. Optionally a couple of stick grenades
  on a waist cord. NO chest rig, NO pack.

**Weapon**: satchel charge is the "weapon"; secondary AK-47 slung tight on the back, or
just a pistol/grenades. In-game: satchel in hands, AK on back.

**Build**: the most athletic model — lean muscle, crouched run animation. 1.60 m.

**3–5 m read**: nearly naked, skin tone dominates, satchel bags. Cannot be confused
with anything else on the battlefield.

## 4. FEMALE VC FIGHTER

Historically very common — up to a third of some local-force units; famous in the Mekong
Delta "long-haired army."

**Clothing**
- Same áo bà ba black pajamas as §1 (women's cut is slightly more fitted at the waist,
  same at PS1 scale). `#2e3138`.
- KEY ITEM: **khăn rằn checkered scarf** (§8.2) — black-and-white gingham check, worn
  loosely looped around the neck with the two ends hanging down the chest front, or
  draped over the head under the nón lá. This white-flecked patch at the neck is the
  fastest gender/faction read.

**Hair**
- Long black hair in a single low ponytail or braid down the back — model it; it changes
  the silhouette. Alternative: hair bun at nape under the cone hat. Black `#141414`.

**Headgear**: nón lá conical hat (very common for women) or bare head showing the braid.
Do the braid + scarf, and let the hat be a second female skin if you want two.

**Footwear**: Ho Chi Minh sandals / barefoot.

**Gear**: light — small shoulder bag, canteen, SKS ammo pouches on a waist belt (two small
tan pouches). Female fighters commonly served as village militia, couriers, snipers.

**Weapon**: SKS carbine or M1 carbine (lighter weapons were typical); AK also fine.

**Build**: smallest model, 1.50–1.55 m, slight frame, narrower shoulders.

**3–5 m read**: braid + white-checked scarf on all-dark clothing + smallest silhouette.

## 5. NVA REGULAR INFANTRY

The uniformed professional. Cleanest, most "soldier-shaped" of the six.

**Clothing**
- One-piece color scheme: cotton field uniform, button shirt with two chest pockets +
  matching trousers, worn loose. Two eras:
  - Early war (pre-1967) and officers: **khaki/tan** `#a08a5e`
  - 1967-on standard: **"reed green"** — a gray-sage olive green, more green than tan
    but desaturated: base `#6b7355`, shadow `#525941`, highlight `#828a6a`.
  - Pick reed green as the primary NVA color; it separates them from khaki-mixed VC.
- Shirt worn outside trousers or tucked; sleeves sometimes rolled.

**Headgear — the pith/sun helmet** (§8.3). Non-negotiable identifier.
- Color: khaki `#8f7f58` or matching reed green `#66704f`; small metal star badge front
  center (gold/red dot, 2–3 px).

**Footwear**
- Ho Chi Minh tire sandals were extremely common even for regulars on the trail;
  also black canvas/rubber "Bata"-style ankle boots `#2a2a26`. Sandals are authentic
  and cheaper to model.

**Gear**
- **Chicom 3-pocket chest rig** (§8.1) — same as main-force VC.
- **NVA rucksack**: canvas pack with 3 outer front pockets, tie-toggle fasteners,
  X-frame straps; sits high on the back. Olive/tan canvas `#71684c`. The back hump
  distinguishes marching NVA from VC.
- Metal canteen (painted green or bare alloy) on belt; small entrenching tool;
  rolled tarp/hammock strapped under or atop the pack.

**Weapon**: AK-47 / Type 56, or SKS with folded bayonet.

**Build**: fit, 1.60–1.66 m, upright military posture.

**3–5 m read**: pith helmet dome + single-color green uniform + chest rig + backpack.

## 6. NVA/VC HEAVY WEAPONS CREW (RPG gunner / RPD gunner)

Base body = NVA regular (§5) or main-force VC (§2); swap gear and weapon.

**RPG gunner (B-40/RPG-2 or B-41/RPG-7)**
- Launcher tube over shoulder (RPG-2: thin straight tube + wooden heat guard mid-tube,
  bulbous rocket on the muzzle. Bulb olive/dark green `#4c5240`).
- **Rocket carrier backpack**: NVA-made canvas rack/pack with wooden toggles holding
  2–3 spare rockets vertically — the rocket cones/fins stick UP behind the shoulders
  like spikes. This spiky back silhouette is the read. Canvas `#75694a`, rocket bodies
  dark olive with black cone.
- Sidearm only (pistol) or slung SKS; usually no chest rig (replaced by rocket pack + small
  belt pouch).

**Assistant gunner** (optional 7th skin): standard rifleman (§5) but with the same rocket
backpack + AK — just add the rocket pack to the NVA regular model.

**RPD gunner**
- RPD light machine gun: long barrel, wooden stock, **round drum** under the receiver.
- **Drum pouches**: 1–2 canvas circular drum pouches (~25 cm diameter discs) on a shoulder
  strap hanging at the hip — distinctive round shapes instead of the rectangular chest rig.
  Canvas `#6d6a4f`.
- Otherwise NVA regular uniform + pith helmet.

**Build**: give the RPG gunner the stockiest frame of the set (still ≤1.68 m).

**3–5 m read**: weapon shape itself + spiky rocket backpack (RPG) or round drum pouches (RPD).

## 7. THE ICONIC ITEMS — modeling specs

### 8.1 Chicom Type 56 chest rig (THE item — put on variants 2, 5)
- Flat canvas bib worn high on the sternum, hugging the chest (not hanging like modern rigs).
- **3 large center pockets** side by side, each ~18 cm tall × 8 cm wide × 5 cm deep
  (each holds an AK mag) — model as 3 vertical box bulges with a flap on top.
- Flaps close with **wooden toggle-and-loop** (a small peg — 1 tiny cylinder or just texture).
- **2 small pockets on each outer wing** (4 total) — half-height boxes for grenades/oil kit.
- Straps: X-harness over the back + tie tapes around the waist — at PS1 scale, two straps
  over the shoulders + one waist line texture.
- Color: olive-drab or tan cotton canvas, `#6d6a4f` to `#8c7f5c`, darker flap edges.
- Low-poly: one extruded slab (3 bulges) + 2 wing quads; rest in texture. ~30–50 tris.

### 8.2 Khăn rằn checkered scarf (variant 4; optional on 2)
- ~1 m × 40–50 cm cotton scarf, **black-and-white small gingham check** (~2 cm squares;
  at texture scale a 4–8 px checker). Black `#1a1a1a` / off-white `#d9d5c8`.
  (Regional variants exist in purple/brown-white — stick to B/W for the VC read.)
- Worn: looped once around neck, both ends hanging down the chest to mid-torso; or over
  the head/shoulders. Model: a neck torus + two hanging quads with checker texture.

### 8.3 NVA pith / sun helmet vs US M1 — proportions
- Shape: smooth **dome with a continuous narrow brim all the way around**, brim slightly
  wider and downward-sloped at front and rear. Think "safari helmet," not "pot."
- vs US M1: M1 is a deep hemisphere with almost no brim and a lip. The pith helmet dome is
  **shallower and wider**, and the ~3–4 cm all-around brim reads as a distinct flange —
  the top-down silhouette is an ellipse noticeably wider than the head.
- Proportions: dome height ≈ 0.45 × head width; brim extends ~15–20% beyond the dome edge,
  angled down ~15–20°.
- Construction: resin-impregnated cardboard/cork covered in cotton — perfectly smooth,
  matte. Khaki `#8f7f58` or reed green `#66704f`; texture a small star badge front center
  and a thin chin strap line.
- Low-poly: 8-segment dome + single brim ring, ~40 tris.

### 8.4 Nón lá conical straw hat (variants 1, 4)
- A clean straight-sided **cone**, no dome curvature: diameter ~40–45 cm, height ~18–20 cm
  → half-angle ≈ 50° from the axis (a wide, shallow cone; apex angle ~100°).
- Extends well beyond the shoulders' inner line — top-down it's a big circle.
- Palm-leaf color: pale dry straw `#cbb474`, weathered gray-tan `#b0a075`; texture faint
  concentric rings (the bamboo hoops). Underside slightly darker `#a08c60`.
- **Chin cord**: a thin ribbon/cord from brim edges under the chin — 1 texture line or a
  2-tri strip; often a soft colored ribbon (dark red or black) on women's hats.
- Low-poly: open cone, 8–10 segments, ~20 tris. Tilt it back slightly on the head.

### 8.5 Ho Chi Minh tire sandals — worth modeling?
- Real thing: flat black rubber tire-tread sole + 2 straps across the toes + 1 strap
  around the heel/ankle, all black inner-tube rubber `#1d1d1d`.
- **At PS1 scale: do NOT model straps.** Model a flat dark sole under a bare-skin foot and
  paint the straps in the texture (2 dark lines across the top of the foot). From 3–5 m
  the read is "dark flat sandal + skin," which is exactly right. Barefoot with a darker
  sole texture is also acceptable for variants 1/3/4.

## 9. BODIES, SKIN, SCALE

- **Height**: Vietnamese fighters of the era averaged **1.58–1.65 m**; US troops ~1.75 m.
  In-game: build VC/NVA at **90–93% of the GI model height** — the size gap is historical
  and gameplay-legible. Female VC at ~87–88%.
- **Build**: lean throughout; narrow shoulders, low body fat, ropy forearms. No broad
  GI-style torso. Sapper = defined lean muscle; RPG gunner = stockiest.
- **Skin tones** (sun-exposed field fighters):
  - Base tan `#b5834f`
  - Darker/weathered `#9c6b42`
  - Lighter (NVA regular, less sun) `#c69365`
  - Sapper w/ charcoal smear: overlay streaks `#3a332c`
- **Faces**: black hair `#141414`; keep faces clean-shaven (facial hair was rare).

never color-collides with the khaki-topped main-force VC.
