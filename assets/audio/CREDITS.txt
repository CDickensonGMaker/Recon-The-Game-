RECONgame — AUDIO ATTRIBUTION AND LICENCE LEDGER
================================================================================
Every audio file in this tree that did NOT originate in this project is listed
here with its source and licence. A file that is not listed and not marked
"original" below has no cleared provenance and MUST NOT ship.

This file is .txt on purpose: export_presets.cfg excludes "*.md" from the build,
so a Markdown ledger would be absent from the very artefact it documents.

Last audited: 2026-07-29

4. *** NOT CLEARED FOR DISTRIBUTION — MUST BE EXCLUDED FROM EXPORT ***
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Both are gitignored, which keeps them out of GitHub. It does NOT keep them out
of the exported build: export_presets.cfg uses export_filter="all_resources"
and its exclude_filter names no audio. Unless excluded there, both are packed
into the .pck and shipped.

  assets/audio/ambience/jungle_day.mp3            (55 MB)
      .gitignore:27-28 — "YouTube-sourced dev ambience - license-unclear,
      replace for release". Loaded live at scripts/world/game_world.gd:269.

  assets/audio/Radio Vietnam/music/*.ogg          (14 tracks)
      .gitignore:30-38 — converted from the commercial LP "Music of Viet Nam";
      "we hold no distribution right". Used by the radio prop.
      radio_prop.gd already warns once when absent, and the 2026-07-27 decree
      designed a broadcast-only radio as the intended fallback, so excluding
      these from export matches existing design intent.

  The 5 broadcast .ogg files in assets/audio/Radio Vietnam/ are the Summoner's
  own edits and are a separate question from the LP music above.

NOTE: LICENSE:1-7 currently asserts that all audio in this repository is
