## 1 · Executive summary — the five things that matter

**1. The architecture is not the problem. Two of the three targets already share one build
path, by construction.** `DemoGame` owns only a switchboard and an arc clock; it calls the
shipped `GameFlow` and the shipped `MissionGenerator` (`demo_game.gd:12-20, 97-101`). Demo
divergence is six `demo_mode` branches repo-wide (`game_flow.gd:588`, `field_director.gd:1414`
and `:1459`, `lazy_group.gd:94`, `mission_generator.gd:1018`, `siege_director.gd:174`,
`heli_lift.gd:151`) plus one parallel planner. ADR-028 is being honoured. **The exception is
`ai_stress_arena.gd` — 2,143 lines that build a parallel world** (`TerrainManagerStub:11`,
`FlatHeightmap:23`, `ArenaGrid:33`, `_build_firebase:792` from two kit GLBs instead of
`fsb_main_v3`). That is the one real ADR-028 violation, and he has already asked for it to be
fixed in his own words (quoted at `game_flow.gd:211`).

**2. ADR-019 (Hearts & Minds / village allegiance / VC manpower) is a PHANTOM.** It is named
in `GAME_GUIDE.md:279` as part of THE SLICE — the declared build target — and indexed at
`:396`. Search of `scripts/ tests/ data/ terrain/` for `allegian|hearts|sympath|village_stance|loyal`
returns **exactly one hit, and it is a comment**: `agent_registry.gd:12` ("...or the
hearts-and-minds ledger may ever walk this roster"). There is no ledger. The 2026-08-05
migration decree reached the same conclusion independently (`DEMO_SHIP_BACKLOG.md`, "Ruling
4's system does not exist"), so this is confirmed twice — but the GAME_GUIDE still reads as if
it exists.

**3. Two doc machines are RED right now, and one has regressed since the last audit.**
`python tools/probe_doc_pointers.py` exits 1:
- **15 unpointered assertions against ceiling 12 → FAIL.** (`README.md` 4, `.claude/agents/blender-overseer.md` 3, `.claude/agents/recon-overseer.md` 3, `DESIGN.md` 3, `AGENTS.md` 1, `CHANGELOG.md` 1.)
- **97 broken pointers against ceiling 97 — at the ceiling, up from 95 on 2026-07-28.** The ratchet has never moved down and is now pinned. Worst: `ADR-011` (16), `ADR-012` (13), `ADR-008` (11), `ADR-010` (10). Most name `mission_director.gd`, a file that does not exist — the exact failure mode CLAUDE.md:356 warns about.

**4. Both P1s from the 2026-07-28 audit are still open, untouched.** `production/adr/` still
holds two files numbered ADR-035 (`ADR-035-the-route-the-pencil-and-the-hunters.md` and
`ADR-035-the-siege.md`), so "per ADR-035" does not resolve — and ADRs are the top of the canon

of which exists on disk** (verified by directory listing: 001–023, 025, 026, 028–036).

**5. One verified live gameplay bug, and it silently eats data on the success path.**

`_base_result` body). `fail_mission()` patches it in by hand (`field_director.gd:210`).

**Every patrol you walk back from discards its noncombatant deaths.** Named on 2026-08-05;
still unfixed today.
