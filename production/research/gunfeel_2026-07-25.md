# GUNFEEL RESEARCH — heavy & punchy without the B-shooter stink
**Date: 2026-07-25.** Three-pass research: industry theory (web), Godot 4.7 implementation (web + architect knowledge base), and a channel-by-channel audit of the live stack (file:line below, verified this date). Damage numbers are LOCKED (ADR-016) — everything here is presentation. Renderer is Forward+ by decree; PS2-era GPU budget; PSX restraint.

## The thesis

AAA gunfeel = many small feedback channels firing on the **same frame** (audio layers + camera + viewmodel + world FX + target reaction, inside ~100ms — Swink's correction-cycle bound). The 2000s B-shooter failure = **one channel doing all the work**, usually a single dry sample over a static viewmodel. RECON today: audio channel B+, kinetic channels near-zero. The fix is additive, not surgical.

## Where RECON stands (audit, 2026-07-25)

**Strong foundations — build on, don't rebuild:**
- Recoil parameter set fully live per weapon: first-shot ×1.4, climb/cap, exponential recovery (`weapon_holder.gd:523-535`, `player.gd:1296-1310`, `.tres` fields). ADS halves it, prone ×0.55.
- 3-layer player shot: report round-robin + mech at -8dB + reverb-bus tail (`audio_manager.gd:256-287`), 24-voice pooled 3D bank for AI with distance layers (`:183-252`), Weapons-bus compressor + Master limiter (`default_bus_layout.tres`), ambience duck per shot (`:339-344`).
- Outgoing hit feedback: 4-layer blood, flinch/stagger graded by damage (`enemy_base.gd:2178-2191`), hitmarker, gib thresholds. Suppression-in: screen shader + jitter + Master lowpass (`player.gd:1400-1437`).

**Channels contributing ZERO weight today:**
1. **Viewmodel animation** — `m16_fp.glb` carries `rifle_idle`, `reload`, `reload_empty`, `jam`; only `rifle_idle` ever plays (`weapon_holder.gd:880-882`). Reload and the 4.6s jam-clear are bare timers. **No fire clip exists in the export.** No mechanism plays authored clips from weapon state anywhere in the codebase.
2. **Camera response to firing** — no shake, no kick beyond recoil rotation, no FOV events (`camera.fov` written only by ADS lerp, `weapon_holder.gd:253-257`). A 250-damage rocket 5m away moves the camera 0°.
3. **Movement bob** — none (zero grep hits); gun equally inert standing/walking/running. Sway is time-based sine (`weapon_holder.gd:836-841`), ignores mouse look.
4. **Shell ejection, muzzle smoke** — absent. Flash is two shared billboard quads, no light, same for every weapon (`gun_fx.gd:253-280`; lights banned by ADR-026 — see amendment proposal below).
5. **Supersonic crack** — `is_supersonic` (`weapon_data.gd:83`) referenced nowhere; `crack_1..3.wav` on disk, never played; near-misses add suppression silently (`enemy_base.gd:2536-2554`). Rounds snapping past the ear are SILENT — Hell Let Loose's most-criticized absence, Rising Storm 2's proven strength.

## THE BUILD PLAN — ranked by weight-per-effort

Scene spine that composes without fighting (each layer owns a transform no other layer writes — authored clips keep their bones, procedural lives on parent nodes clips never key):
`Head → CameraRecoil (spring, rot) → ShakePivot (trauma, rot) → Camera3D → ViewmodelRecoil (spring, pos+rot) → FPArms (authored clips)`
All feel systems run on wall-clock `_process` — NEVER SimClock-scaled.

### Phase 1 — free wins (audio + timing)
1. **Per-shot variation hardening**: AudioStreamRandomizer per layer, pitch ±5-8 cents, no-repeat. (Have 3 takes; verify variance is audible.) Cost: FREE.
2. **Sidechain duck**: key the ambience-bus compressor off the Weapons bus so every shot squashes the jungle for a beat. One bus effect. Cost: FREE.
3. **Sub/LFE layer**: offline asset pass — every fire_* gets low-end energy added, mids cut (RS2:V's Muraski rule: never boost, cut). Zero runtime cost.
4. **Frame-0 discharge audit**: bang + flash + kick on the SAME frame as the click, always; the anim retimes around frame-0 discharge, never the reverse (Swink <100ms law).
5. **Supersonic crack wiring**: the near-miss geometry already computed at `enemy_base.gd:2536-2554` plays `crack_1..3` positionally, gated by `is_supersonic` — turning two dead wires into the fear channel. Cost: CHEAP.

### Phase 2 — the kinetic stack (all FREE-class)
6. **Camera kick spring** (CameraRecoil node): the recoil the mouse fights. Two-rate lerp spring — target takes instant kicks + decays at return_speed, current chases at snappiness. Kick fast, recover slower — weight lives in the asymmetry. M16 snappy, M60 long settle, M79 one huge heave.
7. **One trauma accumulator** (ShakePivot): trauma², FastNoiseLite-driven, ROTATIONAL ONLY (±0.5-2°; translational shake = the HLL seasick mistake). Own shot +0.05, M79 launch +0.2, nearby explosion +0.5-0.7. One system serves shots, explosions, and suppression flinch forever.
8. **Viewmodel spring** (ViewmodelRecoil node): replaces the `_punch` decay (`weapon_holder.gd:843-850`) whose impulse currently drowns in the pose lerp — positional shove-back + rise + roll jitter, snap-out/ease-back, per-weapon numbers.
9. **Movement bob + look-lag sway**: gait-synced bob, small rotational drag on mouse input, settle after stopping — the "object with mass, not HUD sticker" tell.

### Phase 3 — authored-animation integration (the M16 payoff)
10. **Clip trigger mechanism**: play `reload`/`reload_empty`/`jam` from weapon state, timer stays authoritative for gameplay (ADR-018 fixed times), clip runs cosmetically alongside; `speed_scale` clip to timer if lengths drift. The 4.6s jam-clear finally SHOWS the authored clear.
11. **Fire clip**: authored fire anim (needs export — none in the current glb), procedural spring layered on top for rate-independence.
12. **Foley beats on anim frames**: mag-out/mag-in/bolt hits synced to the authored beats; last-round bolt lock-back as free ammo feedback (sparse-HUD doctrine).
13. **Combat-corridor audit** (Destiny rule): recoil apex and anim poses must frame the aim point, never cross it.

### Phase 4 — world punctuation (CHEAP, budget-gated)
14. **Muzzle light, player only**: one persistent shadowless OmniLight3D, tight range 3-5m, energy pop 0.05s, ±30% random. **Needs ADR-026 amendment** (pooled-exception clause). AI shooters: emissive card only, or a hard 3-5 light pool for nearest on-screen shooters, blessed only by a mid-firefight perf probe. Dark jungle = maximum payoff per lumen.
15. **Shells + muzzle smoke**: persistent one-shot GPUParticles3D re-armed per shot (never instanced), 4-8 particles, `fixed_fps=30` (cheaper AND chunkier = PSX-correct), no particle collision — shells fade at 1.2s. 4.7's per-axis rotation makes 4 tumbling shells read.
16. **Directional death impulse**: kill impulse along the bullet vector on the ragdoll — with 1-2-shot kills the death IS the hit-confirm (the F.E.A.R. shotgun lesson).
17. **Environment tails**: jungle/clearing/interior tail variants picked by a cheap probe — Vietnam's signature slapback.

### DO NOT BUILD (arcade tells, ruled out by genre)
- Translational screenshake, FOV punch on rifles (explosives only), oversized flash, damage numbers, full-screen hitstop. **`Engine.time_scale` hitstop is a hard NO in this codebase** — it stalls SimClock/AI scheduling and fights the observation instrument; kill-bite, if ever wanted, is a 30-60ms viewmodel-only `speed_scale=0` freeze or a 40ms FOV pinch on explosions.
