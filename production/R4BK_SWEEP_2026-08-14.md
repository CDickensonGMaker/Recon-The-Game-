unfinished, not shipped. Judged against the period-HUD decree

a ruling, not a gap. All pointers verified against code this date. Demo arc = `scripts/levels/demo_game.gd`
booting `GameFlow.demo_mode` (512m, one day, probe@1395s, siege@1440s).

## ABSENT signals, ranked by how often the demo player needs them

1. **The detection pip / player-side stealth state** (row 11) — the entire day arc outside the wire runs
   on the stealth economy (Pillar 3), and at night sappers and infiltrators work the wire; the player has
   zero read on his own detectedness at any point. Everything visible today is the ENEMY's state, not his.
   Already queued as step 24 — this sweep confirms it is the only whole-system r4bk violation in the demo.
2. **The winded flip** (row 2) — every sprint all demo long; the state change at stamina 0
   (`player.gd:1688-1689`) has no sound and no visual, only a speed number the player can't see. One
   breath/pant SFX at the flip (the breath_break.wav pattern already exists at `player.gd:192-199`) would
   discharge it diegetically without touching the decree.
3. **The body-bag stack** (row 13) — once per demo, but it is the canon scoreboard's own missing half:
   `campaign_state.gd:61` promises "STACKED and visible" and nothing renders it; the post-siege compound
   shows the same aid station it booted with. His 7/30 ruling asked for exactly this image.

Lesser notes (not violations): latched HOLD order has no persistent indicator (row 9); ward occupancy
only re-reads at layout time so in-day casualties don't stack (row 13); hunger's single toast then
silence (row 2 sub-note, `player.gd:1110-1112`).
