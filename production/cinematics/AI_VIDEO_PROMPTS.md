# RECONgame — AI Video Prompt Set

Text-to-video prompts describing everything in our two cutscenes (Operation Briefing +
Huey Insertion/Shootdown). Built for short-clip generators (Sora / Veo / Kling / Runway).
Generate each shot separately, then stitch. Prepend the STYLE BLOCK to every shot.

## STYLE BLOCK (prepend to every shot)

> Cinematic Vietnam War film, 1968. Shot on 35mm anamorphic, 2.35:1 widescreen with
> letterbox bars. Muted, desaturated color grade — olive greens, dust browns, hazy pale
> sky. Humid jungle atmosphere, volumetric morning light, drifting haze and smoke, fine

## CUTSCENE 01 — OPERATION BRIEFING

**01A · Establish (wide, slow):** A US firebase in a jungle clearing at first light. A
dirt patch ringed by dense jungle treeline, sandbag emplacements, an open-air tarp hootch,
soldiers moving through the haze. Slow, locked wide shot; morning mist; the base feels
small and exposed in the vast jungle.

**01B · The briefing (medium two-shot):** Under the open hootch, two officers lean over a
map table. One white officer stands quietly smoking a cigar; a Black officer points down
at the map, planning the move. Two radiomen with PRC-25 backpack radios wait nearby.
Static, composed framing, warm lamp light on the map, cigar smoke curling.

**01C · Patrol (tracking):** Two fireteams of US grunts patrol the firebase perimeter in
opposite directions, rifles at the low ready, scanning the treeline. Slow lateral tracking
shot past them; watchful, tense, boots in the dirt.

## CUTSCENE 02 — HUEY INSERTION / SHOOTDOWN

**02-1 · Inbound (establish):** Three olive-drab UH-1 Huey slicks fly in formation, low
over an endless jungle canopy under a hazy morning sky. They start tiny in the distance and
grow closer. High, wide shot over the treetops; rotor thump building; vast negative space.

**02-2 · Approach (low, long lens):** The slicks pass low overhead, rotors beating, sunlit
olive skin, silhouetted against the pale sky, door gunners visible. Low upward angle, long
lens compressing them against the haze; rotor wash rippling the canopy.

**02-3 · VC ambush (through foliage):** From the dark jungle treeline, muzzle flashes
erupt and green-white tracer rounds streak upward at the passing helicopters. Low shot
looking up through leaves; concealed Viet Cong fighters, chaotic sudden fire.

**02-4 · Shootdown (the reveal, held long):** One Huey takes fire, yaws hard, black smoke
trailing from the engine. It banks over, rotor tilting, and spirals down toward the canopy
— heavy, catastrophic, slow. The camera cranes/tracks it down; debris and smoke; the other
two slicks break away.

**02-5 · Landing (wide, dust):** The two surviving Hueys flare nose-up and settle into a
dirt LZ, rotor wash blasting a storm of dust and debris across the clearing. A sandbag
emplacement frames the foreground; jungle wall behind.

**02-6 · Disembark (dynamic):** US soldiers pile out of both sides of the hovering Hueys,
jumping down and fanning out low across the LZ toward the treeline — some dropping to a
crouch and aiming, others advancing at a walk, M16 muzzle flashes as they open fire on the
VC in the jungle. Rotor wash, dust, tracers; the firefight begins.
