# ADR-029 — AMENDMENT B: a world verb is legal; an objective counter is not

**Status:** RATIFIED 2026-07-20 by the Summoner (commissioned directly).
**Amends:** ADR-029 (The Open Patrol Simulator), §4 "No player-facing mission tracking, ever."
**Pillars:** 3 (Freedom — the world answers to the player), 5 (Fail forward — the world remembers).

---

ADR-029 §4 forbids player-facing mission tracking, and §7 kills objective tracking outright. That
prohibition has been read too widely: it has been treated as a ban on the player *doing* anything
consequential, which would leave a patrol simulator in which nothing the player does to the world
survives him. **The line is drawn at bookkeeping, not at agency.** A **world verb** is legal and
encouraged: the player acts on a place, **the world changes and the world remembers** — the tunnel
mouth is gone, the enemy can no longer surface there, and it is still gone next patrol. What
remains forbidden is the **objective counter**: any UI that enumerates, scores, tallies, targets or
progresses those acts — no "tunnels destroyed 3/7", no progress panel, no floating marker, no
checklist, no completion toast beyond the diegetic feedback the act itself produces. The player
must be able to learn what he has done only by walking back and looking. A verb that changes the
world is a game; a verb that increments a number is a quest log.

**Consequences / what is sacrificed:** the player gets no confirmation that destroying a thing
"mattered," and some players will never notice the world changed at all. That is the price of §4
and it is paid deliberately.
