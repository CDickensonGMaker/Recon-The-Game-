## THE HEADLINE FINDING

Between 8/7 and 8/8, four gameplay systems were ruled in and code-completed:
**S27 camp mortar harassment · S28 downed-pilot recovery · S29 destructible jungle · ambient
walking-dice encounters.** All four are in the demo world. **None has ever been playtested.**

In the same window the art critical path did not move: **`fsb_main_v3.glb` is still dated
2026-07-26**, while `chow_hall.blend` (8/3), `firebase_v3.1_RECOVERED_medical.blend` (8/5) and
`mortar_pit_crewed_US_v1.blend` (8/7) all sit stranded in Blender. Sixteen days of finished art has
not reached the game.

**That is the scope problem, and it is not "too many features left to build" — it is verification
debt on features already built, sitting on top of an art bottleneck that has not moved.**

The single highest-value scope action available is not cutting a feature. It is: **put those four
systems behind one EA flag, playtest once, and keep only what survives.** That converts four
unknowns into one bounded decision instead of four open-ended debugging campaigns.
