# Game designer — smaller waves (2026-09-11)

**Read the code, not the plan.** `siege_director.gd`: 45 men = an 11-man probe at 1395 s and ONE
`reinforce(+34)` at 1440 s. `LIVE_CAP 50` never binds on a 45-man night, so every cell crosses the
materialize ring as it arrives — a wall. The press rotation (`PRESS_CYCLE_S 8`) sends one squad
rushing at a time, but with all four squads already at the wire that reads as a wall with a ripple.

**His reference is right about what fear is.** Hell Let Loose never shows you the roster. It shows
five, you win, five more, you're reloading, the third five is the one. Fear is cadence; the
excitement of success needs a wave you *beat*. A wall gives one beat: it arrives, and either the
ratio breaks it or it breaks you.

**What a ramp buys.** Wave one (8) is a fight one rifleman and the garrison win. Wave three (16-20)
with the mortars ranged in is the one that reaches the wire. The total is unchanged — fail-forward's
"death matters" is the same 45 men — but the player now has three chances to feel it coming.

**The risk, named before the numbers.** Piecemeal arrival is piecemeal death. The break ratio
(42.5% of PEAK, ADR-035 §4) is against the whole roll; if waves die one at a time the base holds
more often and the night never crests. That is the measurement the probe has to make.
