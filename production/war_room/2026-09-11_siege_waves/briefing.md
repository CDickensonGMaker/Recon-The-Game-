# BRIEFING — smaller waves for the assault (2026-09-11)

**The Summoner, verbatim:** *"what if we tried smaller waves for the assault ? so its like a ramping
up of intensity because even in hell let loose you see like 5 guys at once and it feels like you're
gonna be over run. so it's finding that balance of lethality and fun to the game, plus having the
missions and stuff. there's a fear of failure and the excitement of success from the combat"* — then
*"do whatever you need to."*

**What it touches.** ADR-035 §1 (cadence), §2 (the marching cell's materialize ring), §4 (the break
ratio and the ledger). The Summoner's own third guard on the AI-LOD work — *an optimisation may not
change who wins the siege* — does not bind here because this is a DESIGN change; instead it sets the
measurement: the change must be shown to change who wins by a measured amount, and the Summoner rules
on that amount.

**Pillars in tension.** P1 believable firefights (a tide of men reads as an assault; a trickle reads
as a queue) · P5 fail forward (the fear needs a real chance of loss; smaller waves killed piecemeal
make the base hold more) · and the laptop, which pays for men who are near, animated and shooting at
once, not men on the roster.

**What already existed.** `LIVE_CAP 50` on materialized men, cells HELD at the ring when the cap is
hit and THAWED as the dead buy room, a press rotation that sends one squad rushing while the others
shoot, and the 45-man night arriving as an 11-man probe plus ONE reinforcement of 34.
