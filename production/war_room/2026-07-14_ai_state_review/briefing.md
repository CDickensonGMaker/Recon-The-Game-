# War Room Briefing — State of AI in RECONgame

**Summoner request:** *"Once those tasks are done, show me the states of all the AI in the game by using the war room. Then let me know when you're ready to transition into a new task."*

**Context:** The 10-part terrain / grunt / animation fix plan has been completed. The next declared task is the AI Combat Stress Test Arena, which ties into squad AI and enemy AI. Before designing the arena, the Council must survey the current AI systems, their states, and any blockers.

**Pillars in play:**
1. **Vietnam, not Valorant** — AI behavior must feel like 1960s jungle war, not arcade waves.
2. **One life, one Operation** — AI should create tension, not cheap death loops.
3. **Squad is the spine** — squad AI must be readable and trustworthy.
4. **Every system measured** — AI behavior must be observable and testable.
5. **Honor the source** — AI tactics should echo Vietnam-era small-unit doctrine.

**Questions for the Council:**
- What AI states exist for allies and enemies?
- Which AI systems are functional, partial, or missing?
- What blockers exist for the AI Combat Stress Test Arena?
- What is the minimal arena design that exercises the current AI honestly?
