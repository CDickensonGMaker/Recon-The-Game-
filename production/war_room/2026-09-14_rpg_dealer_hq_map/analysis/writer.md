## 2 · THE PICKS — four triggered scripted events inside ONE demo day

Read against the 9/13 decree's clock (`../2026-09-13_rpg_45min_hearts_and_minds/synthesis.md`):
06:30 sim boot on the bunk · first walk to the ville ~4–13 min · wire in ~13–17 · hooch row ~17–19 ·
second walk-out (camp / crash) ~19–30 · dusk ~30 · stand-to → probe → assault ~33 on. Each pick names
its trigger as PLACE + STATE + WINDOW, fires ONCE per sim day under a stable id (the ledger's shape,
`hm_ledger.gd`'s `note(id, ...)` idempotency), stages 20–60 s of NPC orders + toast lines, seizes no
camera, and leaves the player free to walk away from all of it. **The test Caleb wrote into Pillar 3
on 9/09 — could he have walked away? — is answered for each.**

### PICK 1 · THE BARRELS — the dealer's first meeting (I4 `fya.05`; I2 p23 *"Tired of burning shit barels"*)

**Trigger.** Player within ~8 m of the burn pad (`fb_burn_barrel` / `fb_latrine` mesh families,
`site_planner.gd:2321-2322`; `"burn"` / `"latrine"` are already `detail` occupations, `:1278`) between
06:30 and ~07:30 sim, on his first pass out of the hooch. Id `event/day1/barrels`.

**The staged beat (~45 s).** Two garrison men on the `detail` occupation are already at it — one
dragging the half-drum out of the latrine's rear hatch, one stirring a lit barrel with a stick;
the smoke column is the first thing the player sees of the base outside his hooch (the comic's
`fya.05` composition, three flame panels). The detail NCO turns as the player passes:

> `DETAIL: NEW GUY. GRAB A STICK. NOBODY LEAVES THIS WIRE TILL THE BARRELS BURN.` (13)

And ~10 s later, from the conex behind the supply dump (the dealer's corner, §3), unprompted, the
dealer — who does not get up:

> `POTEET: COUSIN. YOU DON'T WANT THAT DETAIL. COME SEE ME. EVERYTHING HAS A PRICE, EVEN THE BARRELS.` (16)

**What the player can do (freedom).** (a) Walk on — the detail NCO says nothing more; nothing
happens; the squad moves out at T+10 s regardless (`demo_game.gd:546`). (b) Go to Poteet: his first
ask is on the table (§3). (c) Stand there: the detail finishes without him. **There is no work verb
in the game and this beat must not invent one** — the barrels are a STAGE for two claims on the new
man (HQ's detail, the dealer's price), not a chore. The comic never shows Michael burn a barrel; it
shows a veteran say he is tired of it.

**Touches.** The DEALER chain opens here (first meeting). The HQ chain is present as the detail NCO —
HQ's claim on the new man is a work detail, which is exactly ADR-038's "the black market pushes the
draftees, and you, toward itself." No H&M deed; the ledger is untouched (the firebase is not a
village).

**Cost.** Two `detail` men with a work marker at the burn pad (a Tier-A marker, ADR-041 §4 — the
burn-pad markers may already exist; the technical lens verifies); two toasts; one once-per-day id.
No new animation is required: the stir is a `detail` stand with a prop; the drag is optional.

### PICK 2 · THE CROSSING — the log, the fall, and the man on the far bank (I3 pp13–14; the rifle that never fires)

**Trigger.** The squad's route to the ville crosses the stream (an authored place in the briefing's
list — "ambush stream"). Fires when the point man reaches the log/ford with the squad bunched behind
him, ~08:00–08:30 sim, first walk-out only. Id `event/day1/crossing`.

**The staged beat (~40 s).** Point man crosses; the second man slips off the log and goes in to the
chest (I3 p14 verbatim on the page):

> `KID: SHIT, THIS LOG IS SLIPPERY.` (5) · `KID: WHOA. I'M GOING IN.` (5)
> `MCCLEARY: GET HIM OUT. AND GET HIS RIFLE OUT FIRST.` (10)

While the squad is bunched laughing at the crossing — **this is the informer's window.** The ville's
runner (`civilian.gd:531-546`: the informer must SEE the player, then a 25 s clock) is placed at the
far bank's tree line by the site plan, a boy with a basket, not armed, and he SEES the squad here.
Levi, unprompted:

> `EYES: SOMEBODY ON THE FAR BANK. NO WEAPON. HE'S SURE IN A HURRY THOUGH.` (13)

Then nothing. He walks off toward the ville. **The rifle in the foreground brush that never fires
(I3 p13) becomes the boy on the far bank who never shoots — the same panel, the same dread, and this
one the demo can afford, because the sniper is post-demo.**

**What the player can do (freedom).** Shoot him (a `civ/<village>/<name>/killed` deed — the ledger's
third producer, and the squad saw it). Chase him (he runs; catching him inside 25 s stops the
`informer/talked` deed — the decree's own `open` fact). Let him go (the ville talked; no dusk
warning; the stand-to waits for the probe's own shout). Or cross elsewhere and never bunch at the
log — the boy sees nothing, and that is freedom, not a bug.

**Touches.** The H&M ledger's hinge, STAGED so a player can see the fork he is choosing at — the
9/13 decree already built the fork; this puts a face on the far bank of it. HQ chain: HQ's dusk line
is present or withheld by this (the decree's consumer). Dealer: none directly — but Poteet's "my
guy from the ville" (§3) is a man who walks this same trail.

**Cost.** One authored stream crossing with a log prop and a far-bank tree line marker for the
informer's stand; a slip animation for one squad man (a fall-and-stand; if it slips, cut the fall and
keep the lines — the beat is the boy, not the log); four toasts.

### PICK 4 · THE WIRE AT DUSK — flares, and *"I swear I saw skeletons"* (I1 pp16–17)

**Trigger.** Stand-to. The first illumination flare pops over the wire (the existing siege's air
beats), before `PROBE_AT_S`, with the player at the wire. Id `event/day1/skeletons`.

**The staged beat (~20 s).** The man in the next position — the earned companion if he came, a
garrison man if not — says, under the flare, exactly what the page says:

> `MCCLEARY: I SWEAR I SAW SKELETONS OUT IN THE BUSH.` (9 — verbatim from I1 p16)

Nobody answers. The flare burns out. Then the sentry's shout or the probe.

**Why this one.** It is the comic's calibration point and its tonal rule in one line: **a man's
report of a vision, with nothing behind it** — the horror as the viewer's state, spoken, never shown,
never confirmed. It costs one toast. It is the ONLY way the Reboot's register can enter the demo
without a horror system, and it obeys the author's rule perfectly because the game draws nothing.

**What the player can do.** Look. There is nothing there — or there is a probe man crawling, which
the player may or may not see, and which is the same ambiguity the page has.

**Touches.** None to any ledger; deliberately. One rule for whoever builds it: **the line must never
coincide with an actual enemy the player can then shoot as the "answer."** If the probe is already
in the wire when the flare pops, the line does not fire. A vision that is confirmed is a monster.

## 3 · THE DEALER'S VOICE and THE HQ VOICE

### 3a · The dealer: SSG VIRGIL POTEET, supply

**Who.** A staff sergeant of supply; a REMF who has been on this hill through two rotations of the
line company and intends to be on it for a third. Louisiana — Minden is Michael's town (I1 p2's draft
card), so Poteet is from the next parish over and knows it, which is why every man is "cousin."

**Corner.** The conex behind the supply dump (`fb_supply_dump` mesh family; the quartermaster
occupation already stands at `USSupplyDepot_001/_007`, `site_planner.gd:1217-1218`). A lawn chair in
the conex's shade, a cooler, a transistor radio playing AFVN talk (the ADR-038 §5 substitute for a
needle-drop). He does not go to the chow line; the chow line comes to him.

## 6 · What the scripted-event system needs from the writer's side (so the words fit the machine)

- **A trigger is PLACE + STATE + WINDOW + ONCE.** Every pick above is a marker region (Tier A,
  ADR-041 §4), a state test (informer not yet answered; ZPU standing; flare up and probe not yet
  in the wire), a sim-hour window, and a stable id. The ledger already has the idempotent shape
  (`note(id, ...)`); an event register should copy it, not invent one.
- **A beat is ORDERS + LINES, never a camera.** HOLD / FOLLOW / MOVE_TO (`AllyBase.OrderMode`,
  `ally_base.gd:279`) plus an animation name plus toasts on the existing channel
  (`FieldDirector.toast` → `MissionHUD.show_toast`, 3.5 s + fade). Silence is a beat too (§4 line 3).
- **Lines are text.** No VO exists for any of them and none may be planned for (the asset wall, 9/13
  analysis §0).
- **Every beat has a walk-away.** If the design of a beat cannot say what happens when the player
  ignores it in one sentence, it is a rail. All four above can.
- **The horror rule, as a build rule:** no scripted event may DRAW a vision. It may let a man SAY
  one. The day a stress-driven horror layer exists (his ruling: not now), Pick 4's line becomes the
  cheapest producer it has.

## 8 · Sacrificed and at risk (Law 2)

- **The sniper is absent from the demo**, and the comic's best panel (I3 p13) is transposed onto a
  boy with a basket. It is the honest size for one day; the rifle waits for the AO he needs.
- **Gus is in the squad and says nothing.** Four picks and none is his; his page-turn needs a corpse
  and a second day. In the demo he is a face and a name. He stays a person by not being used.
- **Poteet is a named, stateful, persistent man**, which ADR-038's consequences priced as the
  expensive kind (a talk verb, a corner, a schedule exception, a face). The lines are cheap; the man
  is not. If he slips to a generic quartermaster body under his name, the voice survives.
- **Pick 2's slip animation** is the one piece of motion asked for that does not exist; cut the fall,
  keep the boy.
- **The barrels beat has no verb** and I have refused to invent one. A player who wants to burn the
  barrel cannot; a reviewer may call that a missing feature. It is a deliberate absence — the comic's
  grunt is tired of the barrel, not proud of it.
- **The ZPU "from the ville" line is a claim the game never adjudicates.** That is by design and it
  will read to some players as the game refusing to answer. It is.
- **Champs' rank drift and the "major"** are left unresolved on purpose; both are Caleb's.
