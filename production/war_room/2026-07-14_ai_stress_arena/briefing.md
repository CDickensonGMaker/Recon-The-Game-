# War Room Briefing — AI Combat Stress Test Arena

**Summoner request:** Implement the "RECON Combat AI Stress Test Arena" spec. Use the existing Gore Lab as foundation. This is not a mission or campaign scenario; it is a sandbox to validate whether soldiers behave like soldiers.

**Pillars in play:**
1. **Vietnam, not Valorant** — believable fire-and-maneuver, not arena shooting.
2. **Squad is the spine** — squad must be readable and cohesive.
3. **Every system measured** — arena must produce observable, honest metrics.
4. **Honor the source** — tactics echo small-unit doctrine.

**Key constraints from spec:**
- Gameplay testing over visual quality.
- No procedural terrain focus; simple functional environment.
- Large open combat space with tactical zones: central combat zone, defensive positions, natural cover, village area, firebase area.
- Symmetrical large-scale engagement: US squads (SL, RTO, Medic, Riflemen, Grenadier, MG) vs VC/NVA squads.
- Test goals: squad movement, combat rhythm, suppression, tactical decisions, emergent story moments.
- Final question: *"If I watched this battle without controlling anyone, would it look like a believable military engagement?"*

**Council:** game-designer, systems-designer, ai-programmer, lead-programmer, ux-designer, devils-advocate.
