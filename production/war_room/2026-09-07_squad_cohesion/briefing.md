# BRIEFING — War Room 2026-09-07 · SQUAD COHESION AS A COMBAT MECHANIC

**Status: PHASES 1-2 ONLY.** The Summoner is participating as a speaker in THE DEBATE.
No weaving, no decree, no record beyond the analyses. A finished judgment would cut him out of his own council.

## The Summoner's opening statement (verbatim)

> "I think Brothers in Arms is a great example of the feeling of what combat should feel like in the
> RECONgame project, but since it's a WW2 game the combat in Vietnam is significantly different. Having
> played the WW2 Hell Let Loose and the Vietnam one quite a bit, the difference is the grand war spectacle
> of WW2 with two large armies fighting against each other, where Vietnam is squad vs squad where the
> cohesion of the unit is what wins the fight."

**Origin:** a session modding Brothers in Arms: Road to Hill 30 into a "solo Hell Let Loose" — raising
lethality so rifles one-shot. **The finding that mattered:** the complaint *"I shoot people and they don't
die"* turned out to be an ACCURACY problem, not a damage problem. Hold this as a lens: the felt problem and
the actual mechanism were different systems.

## The question

**Is squad cohesion a first-class combat mechanic in RECONgame, and if so what is it?**

## The Summoner's pre-council read — to be CHALLENGED, not inherited

He read the Five Pillars and the ADR filenames only. He did NOT read the ADR bodies or the 9/06 RPG pivot.
He expects some of this to be wrong and values a refutation over agreement.

1. Pillar 1 already mandates the BiA/HLL feel — the Brothers in Arms inheritance is canon, not a new import.
2. Pillar 2 already solves the WW2-spectacle problem by putting the grand war at the AMBIENT layer while the
   actual fight stays squad-sized.
3. THE GAP: Pillar 4 "the squad is the RPG" is about ATTACHMENT. Cohesion is about WINNING FIGHTS. You can be
   deeply attached to a squad with no tactical coherence. No ADR appears to cover this.
4. BiA cohesion is ORDER-driven (Find/Fix/Flank/Finish). Vietnam cohesion is SOP-driven — react-to-contact
   fires in three seconds without orders. What the player commands is the RECOVERY, not the reaction.
5. BiA's loop starts at Find/Fix. In Vietnam you BEGIN already fixed — the enemy chose ground, time, and
   whether the fight happens at all.
6. Possible shape: cohesion as a resource the ambush SHATTERS and drills RESTORE, gating which orders
   function. This would make the Fairness Law's near-miss load-bearing rather than merely merciful — it is
   the window in which cohesion can be recovered rather than only lost.

## Binding constraints every architect must answer against

- **The Five Pillars.** Especially 3 (Freedom — no rails, ever) and 5 (Fail forward).
- **The r4bk Law** — a feature without a visible HUD affordance does not exist. BUT note ADR-018 §2 set a
  precedent: silent behavioural squad XP "violates the r4bk law on purpose — its affordance is the man
  himself." Whether cohesion may claim that same exemption is a real question, not a given.
- **ADR-018** — rank gates AUTHORITY, never ABILITY. Player stats KILLED. No progression touches accuracy,
  recoil, sway, handling, health, stamina.
- **ADR-016 / ADR-040** — one damage grammar, flat base x zone, no parallel damage path. ADR-040 exists to
  refuse a sympathetic attack on Pillar 1 and names a real hole: the first-shot near-miss triggers on the
  SHOOTER ENTERING COMBAT, not on the player being unaware, so a man already fighting your squad who swings
  onto you spends no warning shot.
- **ADR-021** — FOLLOW then LEAD is the tutorial; the follow phase is named as "the closest this design comes
  to a rail" and "may never be enforced."
- **ADR-012** — squad orders are FOLLOW/HOLD/MOVE-TO/FIRE-TOGGLE, dual-bound F1-F4 + C/H/X/N. Neither
  binding may be removed. Four prime keys already spent.
- **ADR-029** — the open patrol simulator. No mission tracking, no objective markers, diegetic pointers only.
- **THE GATE (ADR-015 / GAME_GUIDE §8.0)** — THE DEMO PLAYTHROUGH is the session entry gate and is
  UNDISCHARGED. Feature epics are blocked. Exempt: bug fixes, presentation for shipped systems,
