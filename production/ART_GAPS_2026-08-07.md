## 2026-08-07 — DESTROYED-BUILDING MESHES (new gap, opened by his explosives-only ruling)

Village buildings are now damageable (`site_planner.gd` FSB_STRUCTURE_KINDS, `nha_tranh_` /
`nha_san_` / `nha_ruong_`). But `Destructible._do_destroy` (`destructible.gd:73-87`) hides the

`destroyed_bunker.glb`, `rubble_heap_tall.glb`, `rubble_pile.glb`. The explosion plays on the

`explosion_napalm`. `test_destructible` now fails if any mapped kind stops yielding a mesh.

## 2026-08-07 — NO `__mg` WEAPON-FAMILY CLIPS (found booting the game)

Verbatim from the boot:

```
[MODEL] no '__mg' weapon-family clips in anim_library - those carriers hold their
weapons like rifles (first miss: idle_aiming__mg)
```

`model_actor.gd` resolves clips by weapon family (`__mg`, `__smg`, …) and falls back to the rifle
set when a family is absent. `anim_library.glb` carries `reloading__smg` but **no `__mg` family at
all**, so every M60 and RPD carrier — US and NVA — aims, idles and moves holding a machine gun the
way a man holds an M16.

**Visible on both sides in the demo**: the firebase garrison has MG men, and `nva_mg` spawns in the
patrol world. Cost is animation time, not modelling. Not blocking; nothing errors, it just reads wrong.
