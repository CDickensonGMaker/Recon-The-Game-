## The spine, as structured

**MICHAEL** — the fresh replacement. Issues 1–2 establish him. A Selective Service card on the
Issue 1 title page reads **Michael Lee Crawford, b. 14 Aug 1950, Minden, Louisiana, registered
17 Aug 1967** — seventeen at registration.

**THE WW1 JOURNAL.** Michael reads **his grandfather's WW1 journal** while in Vietnam. Explicit
parallels between the characters across time. This is the structural spine of the series, not a
decoration. NOTE: `old comics\ww1` exists in the same Drive folder — check whether the grandfather's
war is already drawn.

**EUGENE** — arrives in Issue 2. "Really weird" on arrival, and becomes "a real morbid":
- cutting ears off the dead and wearing them around camp
- found one day holding a head in his lap, which he had been carrying in his bag
- the SERGEANT strikes him for it; Eugene runs from camp

**THE SKULL-FACED SNIPER** — Eugene is kidnapped by him. A **Russian WW2 veteran** who survived a
bombing raid that disfigured his face. An elite sniper operating in the area. He "starts to torture
on the squad" — an ongoing predatory presence, not a single encounter.

**ISSUE 4 (drawn, unwritten):**
- the aftermath of Eugene's capture; he escapes and returns to HQ
- but the rumour has already spread that **he was eating people**
- Eugene has a meltdown, **steals the major's pistol and shoots him in the face**
- a mental breakdown, crying, going crazy; "smoozing this nurse"
- he is loaded onto a plane **strapped down on a cart, screaming as he goes up**
- then it cuts to the skull-faced sniper — **and we really see him for the first time**

**"and thats about as far of a story i had written overall."** Everything past that plane is open.

## Why this matters to RECONgame

Recorded as observation, not decision. The council rules.

- **Michael's replacement arc is a mechanic the game already half-owns.** The Huey delivers
  replacements mid-siege; the 2026-09-07 cohesion council found Vietnam's real cohesion failure was
  the individual rotation policy, and that Pillar 4 promises men who "rotate home" while no rotation
  clock exists anywhere in the code. The comic is about the thing the game promises and has not built.
- **The skull-faced sniper is a recurring antagonist, not a boss.** "Starts to torture on the squad"
  is a persistent hunter in an open AO — which is what Pillar 3 (any route, any order) is built for,
  and it needs no rails.
- **The draft card is a character sheet.** Name, DOB, hometown, height, weight, eye colour, drawn as
  a period document. Pillar 4 asks for "minimal stats, maximal attachment"; this is that, in the
  author's own art. When a man dies you are left holding his card.
- **The horror escalates through a PERSON, not a monster.** Eugene's decline is the horror in Issues
  2–4; the worms are the register, not the plot. That is the tonal rule the Reboot cover already
  states by omission — two Hueys and a man in the grass, no worms anywhere, where the pilot cover led
  with them.
