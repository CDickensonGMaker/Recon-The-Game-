## 4 · THE ADR-029 VERDICT (this is blocking a feature the Summoner asked for)

### What ADR-029 actually forbids

**§4:** *"No player-facing mission **tracking**, ever… Floating objective markers are forbidden."*
**§7:** *"Objective tracking dies."* — in context, the sentence immediately follows the list of what
survives (toast bus, fire support, escalation, flags, squad wiring) and immediately precedes *"The
briefing/offer/select/exfil-bird chain is deleted."*

**ADR-029 forbids a BOOKKEEPING LAYER, not a WORLD VERB.** Specifically it forbids: an objective
panel or quest log; floating world-space markers; offer cards; a `required_mask` that gates whether
you are permitted to leave; and a debrief that scores you on objectives completed.

### What the code actually does

The bookkeeping layer is **intact but dead**, in `scripts/missions/mission_state.gd`:
`:7-10` `objective_titles` / `required_mask` / `optional_mask` / `met_mask` · `:21 register_objective()`
· `:30 complete_objective()` · `:42 is_exfil_unlocked()` · `:109-110` `build_result()` still emits
`objectives_done`/`objectives_total`.

**`register_objective()` has ZERO production callers.** Its only caller is its own test,
`tests/test_mission_state.gd:17-19`. It is a textbook ADR-023 fossil kept alive by its test.

It is **not fully inert**, and this is the real violation: `scripts/ui/screens/debrief.gd:30` still
computes `var score: int = int(r.objectives_done) * 100`, and `:18`/`:50`/`:71` read the counters.
A scoring path keyed to objective completion is exactly what §4 forbids, and it is live.

### The ruling

> **ADR-029 does not forbid the village-completion verb. It forbids counting it.**
>
> Blowing a tunnel mouth, burning a cache, or clearing a village is a **thing you do to the world**.
> The world changes and remembers. That is the north star — *"i just wanna leave the camp and go find
> problems."* What ADR-029 kills is the HUD saying **"OBJECTIVE 2 OF 3 COMPLETE."**
>
> **Buildable today:** a satchel charge with a diegetic prompt, a real explosion, a tunnel mouth that
> is permanently gone, and consequences that bank at the wire. The r4bk Law requires a visible
> affordance — a world-space interact prompt satisfies it and stays diegetic.
> **Forbidden:** a completion counter, a progress panel, a floating marker, or an exfil gate.

**The agent that refused today was wrong on the verb and right to be careful.** The correct move is
bead `v82k`'s own instinct — a one-paragraph ADR-029 amendment stating *"world verbs are not mission
tracking; what dies is the counter"* — then build it. That is a five-minute amendment, not a council.

**Two caveats the Summoner should know before signing:**

1. `GAME_GUIDE.md:262` blesses satchels, **but the guide is pre-pivot and outranked by the ADR**, so
   it is not the authority here. The ADR reading above is what clears the work — not the guide line.
2. **No player satchel exists in code today.** `grep -ril satchel` returns only
   `scripts/enemies/sapper_charge.gd` (VC sappers at the wire) and `hitzone_builder.gd:46`. This is
   greenfield, not a repair.
