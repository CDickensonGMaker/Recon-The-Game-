## Open defects carried out of the 2026-07-28 session

1. **M60 first-person viewmodel is unusable.** `--mg-probe` measured its `MuzzlePoint` at
   ~50 m from the camera: `muzzle=(-70.90, 11.35, -14.71)` vs `cam=(-31.8, 1.7, 15.5)`.
   Cause: `data/weapons/m60.tres` has `hip_position = Vector3(0,0,0)`, so nothing cancels
   the armory-ruler export offset (compare `ak47` `-8.01` Z, `ppsh41` `-27.96` Z). The gun
   fires correctly — rounds simply spawn 50 m away. Owner is benching it.
2. **RPD, Mosin, M70, M1911 share the M60's near-zero `hip_position` profile** and have not
   been verified. M16A1 is also near-zero and demonstrably works, so small values are not
   automatically wrong — but the set is unproven.
3. **All `fire_*_dist.wav` renders are still synthesized.** Near shots on M16A1/CAR-15/AK/
   RPD/Mosin are now real recordings (Snake pack); their distant layer is not.
4. `car15`, `shotgun`, `m26_grenade` have no fire audio — generic `shot_rifle.wav` fallback.
5. **`HOT_CAP = 12`** (`scripts/enemies/enemy_squad.gd`) is a *global, enemies-only* budget —
   `request_hot` is called from exactly one site, `enemy_base.gd:605`. Allies never tier.
   More bodies therefore does **not** mean more fighting AI; cold men can only pick
   `ENGAGE_TARGET` or `HOLD_POSITION`.
6. **`LIVE_CAP = 50`** (`siege_director.gd`) — raised from 18 by owner ruling, **unmeasured**.
7. **Ragdoll cap effectively removed** (`model_actor.gd`, `MAX_ACTIVE_RAGDOLLS` 8 → 256) —
   also unmeasured. Fixes corpses standing up, cost unknown.
8. **Allies now route on the navmesh** via the new shared `scripts/ai/nav_router.gd`.
   Verified only in the arena. Squad-following through the real world is untested, and
   outside a `NavBaker` box allies still steer direct by design.
9. **Mounted-gun asset was aimed backwards** — the old `m60` inside `m60_pintle.glb` had its
   muzzle on Blender −Y (= Godot +Z) against `MGEmplacement`'s "local −Z is downrange".
   Rebuilt 2026-07-28 with the new mesh facing correctly; `GunnerPos` (7.7 m out of place,
   read by nothing) dropped.
10. **Emplacement draw calls doubled**, 8 → 16 materials, tris 1,112 → 10,652, from the
    third-party M60. Tri count is not the concern; the 16 draw calls per nest are.
