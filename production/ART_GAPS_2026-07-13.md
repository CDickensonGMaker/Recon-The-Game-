## 2 · ⚠ **THERE IS NO NVA MODEL. THE NVA ARE WEARING VC BLACK PYJAMAS.**

Measured straight out of `data/enemies/*.tres` → `sprite_unit`:

| Archetype | The model it actually loads |
|---|---|
| **`nva_regular`** | **`vc_guerilla_ppsh`** ← *a Viet Cong guerilla* |
| **`nva_rpg`** | **`vc_guerilla_rpg`** ← *a Viet Cong guerilla* |
| `vc_farmer` | `vc_guerilla` ✓ |
| `vc_rifleman` | `vc_guerilla_mosin` ✓ |
| `vc_sapper` | `vc_guerilla_rpd` ✓ |

**This is the single biggest visual lie in the game**, and it undercuts a mechanic you *just* shipped:
NVA regulars have **determination 0.90** — they hunt you for **84 seconds** and push the net **169m** —
where a VC farmer quits at 41s. **The player has no way to tell them apart.** The thing chasing him and the
thing that gives up look **identical**.

**An NVA regular is a completely different silhouette:** pith helmet or sun helmet, khaki/green uniform,
webbing, chest rig, sandals. **It is the highest felt-value model on this entire page** — because it makes
a system you already own *legible*.
